\section{Summary of Key Findings}

\subsection{Dataset-Level Findings}
The cleaned analytical dataset contains 53,187 posts and 30 variables. Missing body text is substantial (\(53.49\%\)) and was handled as structural missingness rather than random data loss. Duplicate and timestamp integrity checks reported zero duplicate IDs/full rows and zero invalid timestamps in raw input.

\subsection{EDA Findings}
The EDA results show that:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Raw \texttt{score} and \texttt{comms\_num} distributions are highly heavy-tailed.
  \item Log-transformed engagement variables are more analytically stable and interpretable.
  \item Engagement is strongly associated across voting and comment activity (Spearman \(\rho = 0.7852\), \(p \ll 0.001\)).
  \item Post-type engagement differences are statistically significant (Kruskal-Wallis \(H = 5855.658\), \(p \ll 0.001\)).
  \item Extreme outliers are infrequent but non-negligible (\(|z| \ge 3\) rate \(\approx 0.173\%\)).
  \item Temporal effects are visible by posting hour, day-of-week, and event periods.
  \item Post type composition is dominated by text and image formats.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Feature-Engineering Findings}
Controlled ablation indicates that sentiment alone did not improve thresholded classification in this configuration, while adding topic features improved \(F1@0.5\) from \(0.876950\) to \(0.884615\) (absolute lift \(+0.007665\)). This indicates semantic topic context contributes incremental decision-quality utility for borderline viral cases.

\subsection{Interpretive Summary}
The combined evidence suggests that retail attention in WSB is concentrated around high-visibility posts and event-driven spikes. Most posts receive limited interaction, while a small subset drives disproportionate community engagement, consistent with viral attention dynamics in online financial discourse.
